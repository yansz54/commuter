One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not
exist — the one most likely to make a bored commuter smile.

> In Hailston: hail the size of peas. The peas are safe. You are not.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`. See `METHOD.md` for how it
was chosen: 30 candidates, verified by script, single-elimination pairwise
tournament against the published judge criteria.
